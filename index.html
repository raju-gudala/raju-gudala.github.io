About

Hardware first, application second.

I'm an Embedded Hardware Design Engineer working across the full lifecycle of electronics development — schematic capture, component selection, multilayer PCB layout, simulation, bring-up and validation. Most of my time lives inside KiCad and Altium, moving between microcontroller breakout boards and complex, high-voltage power electronics.

My deepest project experience has been in UAV avionics and power systems at TiHAN-IIT Hyderabad — including a custom ArduPilot-compatible flight controller built around the STM32F427, and a tethered drone power system pushing 2.4kW across a 360V DC link. But the underlying skills — signal integrity, power delivery, EMI-aware layout, firmware bring-up — transfer directly to industrial, automotive, or consumer embedded systems.

I'm actively looking to broaden that exposure beyond aerospace, and I pick up new domains quickly with a solid base in communication protocols, embedded firmware integration, and design-for-manufacturing practices.

REV 1.0

UAV Flight Controller — Ti-Circle v1

6-layer PCB · 250+ components · STM32F427

Designed a custom flight controller from scratch supporting ArduPilot firmware, built around the STM32F427 as primary MCU.

Integrated an STM32F103CBT6 as a dedicated I/O co-processor for PWM generation and peripheral management, with IMU sensors over SPI/I2C and telemetry/GPS over UART.

Designed the onboard power supply with multiple LDO and switching regulator stages for clean, regulated rails to every critical peripheral.

REV 1.1

5V Power Module for Avionics

LT8645SHV-2 · 3S–12S LiPo input

Designed a high-efficiency, wide-input 5V DC-DC power module supporting 3S–12S LiPo input using the LT8645SHV-2 synchronous buck converter.

Built in reverse-polarity protection, UVLO, soft-start and power-good monitoring for reliable delivery to critical loads.

Optimized the PCB layout for low EMI, low output ripple and improved thermal performance.

LT8645SHV-2LTspiceLow-EMI Layout
REV 1.2

Carrier Board — Cube Orange+ Flight Controller

Flight-tested on drone platform

Designed a custom carrier board for the Cube Orange+ module, interfaced via board-to-board connectors for full autopilot peripheral integration.

Ran an EMI/EMC-aware layout with controlled impedance routing, ground plane management and signal isolation between high-current and sensitive paths.

Owned the complete flow — schematic, layout, DFM review, Gerber generation — then debugged and validated the fabricated board through bench and flight testing.

Cube Orange+EMI/EMC LayoutFlight-Tested
REV 1.3

Tethered Power System

230V AC → 360V DC @ 8A → 24V DC @ 100A (2.4kW)

Engineered end-to-end power delivery for a tethered platform: an AC-DC base station converting 230V AC to 360V DC @ 8A, and an isolated air-unit converter stepping that down to 24V DC @ 100A.

Added voltage/current monitoring on the ground unit for real-time telemetry, plus automatic arbitration for seamless failover between tether power and an onboard backup battery.

Used TDK power modules for the high-reliability power stage, validated with custom SPICE models, and ran schematic capture and PCB design in EasyEDA.

2.4kWEasyEDAFailover Arbitration
