About

Full-cycle hardware, board by board.

I'm a hardware engineer working across the full lifecycle of electronics development — schematic capture, component selection, multilayer PCB layout, simulation, bring-up and validation. Most of my time lives inside KiCad and Altium, moving between microcontroller breakout boards and complex, high-voltage power electronics.

Over the past 1.5 years at TiHAN-IIT Hyderabad, I've taken UAV flight controllers and power systems from a blank schematic sheet to soldered, flight-tested hardware — including a custom ArduPilot-compatible flight controller built around the STM32F427, and a tethered drone power system pushing 2.4kW across a 360V DC link.

I'm comfortable moving fast across unfamiliar domains, with a solid base in communication protocols, embedded firmware integration, and design-for-manufacturing practices.

UAV Flight Controller — Ti-Circle v1

REV 1.1

5V Power Module for UAV Avionics

LT8645SHV-2 · 3S–12S LiPo input

Designed a high-efficiency, wide-input 5V DC-DC power module supporting 3S–12S LiPo battery input using the LT8645SHV-2 synchronous buck converter.

Built in reverse-polarity protection, UVLO, soft-start and power-good monitoring for reliable delivery to flight-critical avionics.

Optimized the PCB layout for low EMI, low output ripple and improved thermal performance, stabilizing power to flight controllers, GPS, telemetry and sensors.

Carrier Board — Cube Orange+ Flight Controller

Flight-tested on drone platform

Designed a custom carrier board for the Cube Orange+ module, interfaced via board-to-board connectors for full autopilot peripheral integration.

Ran an EMI/EMC-aware layout with controlled impedance routing, ground plane management and signal isolation between high-current and sensitive paths.

REV 1.3

Tethered Drone Power System

230V AC → 360V DC @ 8A → 24V DC @ 100A (2.4kW)

Engineered end-to-end power delivery for a tethered platform: an AC-DC base station converting 230V AC to 360V DC @ 8A, and an isolated air-unit converter stepping that down to 24V DC @ 100A for avionics.

Added voltage/current monitoring on the ground unit for real-time telemetry, plus automatic arbitration for seamless failover between tether power and an onboard backup battery.

Used TDK power modules for the high-reliability power stage, validated with custom SPICE models, and ran schematic capture and PCB design in EasyEDA.
